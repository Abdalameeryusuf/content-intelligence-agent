## A caution for synthesis

His view counts vary by three orders of magnitude within the same format and creator
(3K to 8.6M). That variance is the most useful thing about this catalog — it allows
same-creator, same-format comparison of what worked against what didn't.

It also means **a high view count is not evidence a technique is good.** The breakout
era coincides with broadly popular subject matter (Oppenheimer, Taylor Swift). Subject
choice and technique are confounded, and craft insights must say which one they are
claiming.
