## Observations from the sample

**Yield is concentrated, and not where views are.** One 13-minute video produced 4 of 16
insights. The two highest-view videos in the sample (8.6M, 7.4M) produced 2 and 0
respectively.

**View count did not predict usefulness.** The most transferable craft insight came from
a 29K-view video; the most important strategic insight from a 3.3K-view video.

**Six of twenty produced no standalone insight**, as `references/extraction.md` §4
anticipated. Four of those still contributed evidence to cross-video insights, which is
the extraction guide working as intended rather than wasted spend.
