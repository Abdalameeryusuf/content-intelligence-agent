## What holds across the set

**Openings never begin with framing.** In every sampled video the first sentence carries
a fact, a number, or an outcome. Not one opens by describing what the video will cover.

**Specificity is the recurring mechanism.** Named people, exact figures, real artifacts.
The patterns that work do so because a detail is too specific to dismiss; the ones that
would fail without it are the ones relying on superlatives.

**Structure is declared, not implied.** Where there is a list, its length is stated
("fourth on the list", "three buckets", three observations). The viewer always knows the
shape of what they are in.

## What the performance data does and does not support

Views in the sample range from 3K to 8.6M for the same creator using the same formats.

[INFERENCE] **Technique does not explain that spread.** The breakout instances coincide
with broadly popular subjects — Oppenheimer, Taylor Swift, Neymar. The most
*transferable* patterns came from videos at 29K and 3.3K views.

[INFERENCE] The defensible reading is that technique sets a floor and subject sets the
ceiling. Neither the craft library nor any recommendation drawn from it should treat
high view counts as evidence a pattern is good.

## Gaps

- **No cross-creator validation.** Every pattern here is one creator's habit until Ali
  Abdaal or Chris Raroque is processed.
- **No written-format evidence.** All patterns are extracted from video. Ameer's likely
  formats include writing, and the transfer is inferred, not observed.
- **No audience evidence.** `me/audience.md` is empty, so every "transfers to Lifepal"
  judgment above is `[INFERENCE]` against an assumed audience.
- **Long-form structure is under-sampled.** Only 7 long-form videos exist in the entire
  catalog and the patterns above are dominated by Shorts.

## Agent synthesis

[INFERENCE] The three patterns worth acting on first are **artifact teardown**,
**escalation that demotes the headline**, and **verdict against interest** — because in
each case Ameer already holds the material the pattern needs, rather than needing to
manufacture it. That is the scarce condition; the patterns themselves are not scarce.

[INFERENCE] The pilot's most useful methodological finding is that deliberately sampling
low performers changed the conclusions. Selecting on views would have surfaced the
commercial AI-agent format as the channel's strongest, and recommended imitating a paid
placement. See `kallaway--commercial-content-in-tutorial-form--009`.
